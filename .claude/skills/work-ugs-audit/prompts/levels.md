The score of an item is a share of its max_points. Use only these anchors.

- absent = 0 of max_points: the element asked for is not in the report.
- attempted = 0.5 of max_points: the element is there but largely wrong, or most parts are missing.
- partial = 0.75 of max_points: mostly right, but one part is missing or one technical error remains.
- complete and correct = 0.88 of max_points: every part asked for is present and correct. This is the
  normal good report.
- exemplary = 1.0 of max_points: clearly better than expected. Rare. Only with strong evidence.

Complete and correct is 0.88, not 1.0. A full mark is exceptional.
